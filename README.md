### Engineering Approach

### Architecture

```text

   ↓

   ↓
```

# Engineering Principles

### Simplicity over unnecessary complexity

The best systems are understandable by the next engineer who has to debug them under pressure.

### Observability before optimization

Logs, metrics, and monitoring should explain production behavior clearly.

### Reliability before scale

Systems should fail gracefully before they scale aggressively.

### Product context matters

Good engineering decisions support users, teams, business goals, and long-term maintenance.

### Build for operability

Deployment, rollback, debugging, and recovery paths matter as much as feature delivery.
